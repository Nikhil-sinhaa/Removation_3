# 🏠 Removation.ai

> AI-Powered Interior Design & Renovation Platform with Vastu Shastra Intelligence

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/yourusername/removation-ai)

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 AI Design Engine
- **32 Room Types** - Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, offices, and more
- **400+ Design Styles** - Modern, minimalist, Indian, Scandinavian, etc.
- **Real-time Rendering** - Generate photorealistic designs in <15 seconds
- **Before/After Comparison** - Interactive slider to compare transformations

### 🕉️ Vastu Shastra AI (Exclusive)
- **0-100 Vastu Score** - Instant analysis of room layouts
- **120+ Vastu Rules** - Comprehensive traditional knowledge base
- **Auto-fix Suggestions** - AI-powered recommendations for Vastu compliance
- **Direction-based Analysis** - Room orientation and element placement

### 💰 Renovation Cost Calculator (Exclusive)
- **Real-time Estimates** - Instant cost breakdown in ₹ and $
- **Material Pricing** - 2025 rates for Indian cities
- **Labor Costs** - Region-specific contractor rates
- **Tax Calculation** - GST and other applicable taxes

### 🛠️ Advanced Tools
- **Inpainting** - Modify specific areas of your design
- **Object Removal** - Remove unwanted furniture/objects
- **Fill Empty Room** - Add furniture to empty spaces
- **Sketch to Render** - Convert hand-drawn sketches to 3D renders
- **Outdoor Spaces** - Gardens, patios, and exteriors
